# Client Feedback: Katahdin Trail & Paddle

**Website:** https://husson-webdesign.github.io/ibambe-P4-KatahdinTrail/

Hello,

Thank you for sharing the first version of our website. I like the welcoming colors, the clear navigation, and the friendly introduction to the Katahdin and Millinocket area. The guided trip descriptions are especially helpful: visitors can see the duration, experience level, age recommendation, and price before choosing an activity. The reminder to pay attention to weather and safety is also a thoughtful addition.

For the next version, I would like the site to feel like a complete outdoor adventure experience. Visitors should be able to explore our options, compare activities, understand what to expect, and prepare an inquiry with confidence. Please use the requests below to guide your redesign.

## Phase 1: Clarify Our Story and Content

### 1. Make the homepage a more complete introduction

The homepage currently introduces the business and offers links to rentals and guided trips. I would like visitors to see a little more of what makes our company a good choice before they leave the page.

Please add a short introduction to our two main services: equipment rentals and guided trips. Feature a few activities and give visitors a clear way to explore the full selection. A short section about our approach to helping beginners feel comfortable would also be welcome.

Please make it clear that we serve the Katahdin and Millinocket region and use a welcoming message that encourages visitors to explore at their own pace.

### 2. Make our story feel personal and trustworthy

The About page explains that we want people to enjoy the outdoors without needing to be experts. I like that message. Please build on it by explaining what visitors can expect from us and how we encourage responsible outdoor experiences.

Use the information provided in the project. If you need details about our history, staff, qualifications, or partnerships, list your questions for me rather than inventing them.

### 3. Make the offerings consistent throughout the site

The Guided Trips page lists Beginner River Paddle, Family Lake Adventure, Sunset Paddle, and Intro Hiking Experience. The interest form offers Trail Hike, Lake Paddle, and Campfire Night. I could not find Campfire Night among the listed trips.

Please make sure the choices on the form match the activities we actually describe. If an activity is not in the approved project information, ask me whether it should be included. Visitors should not have to guess whether a form choice refers to one of our trips or rentals.

## Phase 2: Improve the Look and Feel

### 4. Develop a distinctive outdoor identity

The current design feels friendly and uses a bold orange accent. Please build on that direction with a consistent look inspired by the region's lakes, forests, and trails. Keep the site welcoming to families and beginners.

Use consistent headings, buttons, spacing, and cards across the pages. Make the most important information easy to notice without making every section compete for attention.

### 5. Use images that help visitors imagine the experience

The homepage illustration and activity images introduce the outdoor theme. I would also like imagery that helps visitors picture the experiences we describe, such as paddling on calm water or exploring a trail.

Please choose clear images that fit their spaces without distortion. Use images you have permission to use, and document their sources. If a picture does not show the actual Katahdin region, do not label it as a specific local place unless that is confirmed.

### 6. Improve readability

Please check that text is easy to read over images and colored backgrounds. On the homepage, I found the small line above the main heading difficult to read against the image. Make that introduction more legible, and check the rest of the site for similar issues.

## Phase 3: Navigation and Mobile Experience

### 7. Guide visitors through the site

The main navigation makes the five pages easy to identify, and the logo already takes visitors home. Please keep that clear structure and make it obvious which page a visitor is viewing.

Give each page a helpful next step. For example, the About page could lead to our trips, and a rental or trip card could lead to the appropriate interest form.

### 8. Make the site comfortable on a phone

Many visitors may use a phone while planning their trip. Please test the site on several screen sizes and make sure:

- The navigation is easy to open and use.
- Text remains readable without zooming.
- Images and cards fit the screen.
- Buttons are easy to tap.
- Visitors do not have to scroll sideways.

If a compact menu is needed, it should open and close clearly and be usable with a keyboard.

## Phase 4: Rentals and Guided Trips

### 9. Make rentals easier to compare

I like that the Rentals page shows an image, description, and half-day and full-day prices for each item. Please present this information in a consistent, easy-to-scan format and add a clear next step for someone interested in a rental.

The canoe is currently listed as $35 for both half-day and full-day, and the paddleboard as $30 for both. Please check these figures against the project brief or ask me to confirm them. They may be intentional, so do not change them without confirmation.

Where the information is available, help visitors understand how many people an item accommodates and what equipment is included. Ask me about any missing details rather than creating policies or promises.

### 10. Help visitors choose a guided trip

The four trip descriptions already offer useful details. Please make them easy to compare by showing the same information prominently for every trip: duration, experience level, age guidance, price, and a short description.

Give visitors a way to narrow the choices, such as by paddling or hiking, experience level, or trip length. Let them return to all trips easily, and show a friendly message if nothing matches.

### 11. Explain how to prepare

Please add a short, easy-to-find preparation section covering the information already provided, such as following guide instructions and checking weather conditions. Where the project supplies details, explain what visitors should bring and what is provided.

Do not invent safety guarantees, equipment requirements, meeting locations, or weather policies. Identify any missing details for client confirmation.

## Phase 5: Interest Form and Interactive Features

### 12. Make the inquiry journey honest and clear

The homepage button says “Book a Guided Trip,” but it currently takes the visitor to the trip information page. Please use wording that matches the experience, such as “Explore Guided Trips.” On the trip page, invite visitors to prepare an inquiry for the option they choose.

I understand that this class project is hosted on GitHub Pages and that the form will not send or save information. Please place a clear notice before the form so visitors know it is a demonstration. When they complete it, show a neat preview of their answers and allow them to revise them. Do not say that a message, reservation, or booking was sent or confirmed.

If possible, let visitors choose a rental or trip from its page and have that option appear in the form, while still allowing them to change it.

### 13. Check contact details

The Contact page displays a phone number and an email address. Please confirm these against the project brief and identify them as sample details if they are fictional. Do not replace them with invented contact information.

## Phase 6: Make Our Offerings Easier to Maintain

### 14. Organize activity information for future updates

I would like future updates to prices, descriptions, and durations to be easier to manage. Please keep the information for our rentals and guided trips in a separate **JSON file or files** and use that information to display the offerings on the website.

For this stage, please make sure:

- All three rental options and all four guided trips are included using the approved project information.
- The information displayed on the site matches the information in the file.
- The trip search or filter uses that information.
- Visitors see a helpful message if the information cannot be displayed.
- The project documentation explains where a future developer can find and update the information.

If time permits, add a filter for rentals as well. Please confirm prices before changing them.

## Phase 7: Final Review and Handoff

### 15. Review the finished experience

Before presenting the redesign, please test it as though it were ready for a real visitor:

- Check all pages, links, images, and buttons.
- Navigate the site with a keyboard and make sure the current selection is visible.
- Check that text can be read against its background and that images have descriptions where appropriate.
- Test on phone, tablet, and desktop screen sizes.
- Confirm that the form is clearly a demonstration and does not claim to send or save a booking.
- Check that the rental and trip information loads on the published website.
- Review spelling, prices, contact information, and activity names for consistency.

### 16. Provide a short handoff

Please provide:

- The published website link and project repository link.
- A short explanation of the changes you made.
- Screenshots of the site on a desktop and a phone.
- A list of details that still need my confirmation.
- Instructions for a future developer explaining where the activity information is kept and how to update it.
