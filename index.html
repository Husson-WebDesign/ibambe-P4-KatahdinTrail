About Us

Do you want to explore Maine's lakes, rivers, and trails?

Katahdin Trail & Paddle helps visitors experience the lakes, rivers, and trails of Maine with reliable equipment and approachable guided adventures. Whether you are planning a quiet paddle, a family day on the water, or your first guided hike, our team can help you choose an experience that matches your comfort level and outdoor goals. Our rentals and guided trips are designed to make exploring the Katahdin region easier, safer, and more enjoyable. Your adventure matters the most to us.

Guided Trips

We provide ecperienced guides to help the customers in their adventure

Beginner River Paddle

Participants Learn

  • basic paddle strokes
  • boat control
  • safe entry and exit
  • basic water awareness
  • essential paddling safety

Family Lake Adventure

The trip includes:

  • basic paddling instruction
  • opportunities to explore calm water
  • nature observation
  • rest stops
  • guidance from an outdoor leader

Sunset Paddle

Participants enjoy quieter water and changing evening views while paddling with a guide. Guests should be comfortable spending several hours on the water. Lighting, weather, and water conditions may affect scheduling.

Intro Hiking Experience

Topics may include:

  • trail preparation
  • pacing
  • hydration
  • basic navigation awareness
  • outdoor safety
  • appropriate gear
  • Leave No Trace principles

This is an introductory hiking experience and is not intended to represent a technical mountain-climbing expedition.
