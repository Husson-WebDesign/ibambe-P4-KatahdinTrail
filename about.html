Getting to Know Katahdin Trail & Paddle

Do you want to explore Maine's lakes, rivers, and trails?

Our Story

Katahdin Trail & Paddle was created to help more people experience Maine's outdoor environment without feeling that they need to be expert adventurers.

The Katahdin region offers lakes, rivers, forests, and trails suitable for many levels of outdoor experience. The company provides equipment, practical information, and guided activities that help customers explore these environments responsibly.

Our Approach

The company believes that a successful outdoor experience begins with the following.
Our members help customers choose equipment and activities that are appropriate for their experience level.

  • Appropriate Equipment
  • Realistic Activity Choices
  • Awareness of Weather
  • Knowledge of Basic Safety
  • Respect for Individual Ability
  • Preparation
