GUIDED TRIPS

Explore with an Experienced Guide

Guided paddling and hiking experiences designed for beginners and families.

Outdoor conditions can change quickly. Guests should follow all equipment instructions, guide directions, weather advisories, and safety requirements before beginning an activity.

Beginner River Paddle

  • 2 hours
  • Beginner
  • 10+
  • $55 per participant

A relaxed introduction to recreational paddling for guests with little or no previous experience. Participants learn basic paddle strokes, boat control, safe entry and exit, water awareness, and essential paddling safety.

Family Lake Adventure

  • 3 hours
  • Beginner
  • 7+
  • $65 per participant

A family-oriented guided lake experience with basic paddling instruction, calm-water exploration, nature observation, rest stops, and guidance from an outdoor leader.

Sunset Paddle

  • 2.5 hours
  • Beginner to Moderate
  • 12+
  • $70 per participant

An evening paddling experience scheduled around sunset conditions. Guests should be comfortable spending several hours on the water. Lighting, weather, and water conditions may affect scheduling.

Intro Hiking Experience

  • 4 hours
  • Moderate
  • 12+
  • $75 per participant

A guided introductory hiking experience covering trail preparation, pacing, hydration, basic navigation awareness, outdoor safety, appropriate gear, and Leave No Trace principles. Not a technical mountain-climbing expedition.

Guided trips require advance reservation and are offered throughout the primary operating season.
